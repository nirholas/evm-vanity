✦ Chains

An EVM address is keccak256(pubkey)[12:]. It does not depend on a chain, so one grind gives you the same address everywhere, on networks that do not exist yet included. What differs is the explorer, and which deterministic CREATE2 deployers are present if you later want a contract at a vanity address too.

The table below is not a claim to take on faith. Press the button and your browser asks each chain's public RPC directly, with eth_getCode, and marks every row that disagrees with what this repository says.
