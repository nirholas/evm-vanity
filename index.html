✦ EVM vanity wallet generator

Grind a real Ethereum / EVM wallet whose address starts or ends with characters of your choice, for your agent, treasury, or anywhere a memorable 0x address matters. The result is a normal secp256k1 keypair you can import into MetaMask, ethers, viem or Rabby. Runs entirely in your browser. The private key is never transmitted anywhere.

The base scalar comes from a full 256-bit CSPRNG draw, never a narrowed seed. That is the entire difference between this and the 2022 Profanity disaster, where a 32-bit seed made every ground key brute-forceable. Each match is independently re-derived and checked before it is shown, and the default export is an encrypted keystore.

Pattern

Hex characters only (0-9, a-f). Each extra character is 16x harder. Mixed case (e.g. BeeF) matches the EIP-55 checksum: visually distinctive, and 2x harder per letter.
